Why Single-Shot SaaS Trackers Fail

LLMs are stochastic probability engines. Checking a prompt once at a fixed temperature (Mentioned: Yes/No) is statistical theatre—a brand ranked #1 at T=0.2 frequently vanishes or gets heavy caveats at T=0.8. GEO Radar Pro models citation stability via Atanassov's Intuitionistic Fuzzy Sets.

μ (Membership) +100μ Weight

Quantifies rank-weighted Top-3 citation prominence and direct canonical URL attribution across all 6 buyer-intent prompts (`P01` Category Discovery through `P06` Trust Verification).

π (Hesitation) -20π Penalty

Measures epistemic uncertainty and rank volatility across temperature perturbations (`T = 0.2, 0.5, 0.8`). When your site lacks `/llms.txt` or `Schema.org JSON-LD`, hesitation spikes.

ν (Omission) -35ν Penalty

Captures complete brand omission or direct displacement by named competitors (`μ + π + ν = 1`), combined into IFS Index = clip(round(100μ - 35ν - 20π), 5, 99).
